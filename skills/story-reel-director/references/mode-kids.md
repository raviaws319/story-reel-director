# Mode: Kids Stories

Gentle, original picture-book stories for young children (default ages 3–7; ask if the user mentions older kids).

## Story shape

**Meet the hero → Small problem → Try and feel → Kind solution → Happy ending → Moral**

- One clear hero (a child, an animal or a friendly object) with a name that is easy to say.
- The problem is small and relatable: sharing, fear of the dark, feeling too small, trying something new, being patient, telling the truth.
- The hero solves it through kindness, courage, honesty, patience or teamwork, not luck or magic alone.
- Include gentle repetition and sound words ("pitter, patter", "plip!"); children love them.
- Short sentences (≤ 12 words), simple vocabulary, a warm and calm tone.
- The **last scene must carry a `moral` card** (the runtime rejects the project otherwise). Phrase the moral positively: "Even the smallest helper can make a big difference", not "Don't be useless".
- 60–120 seconds is ideal; bedtime stories can be 3–5 minutes.

## Safety rules (always)

- No violence, weapons, injuries, death, scary monsters, horror, peril that lasts more than a moment, or mean-spirited humor.
- No dangerous behavior a child could copy (climbing high places, playing with fire, talking to strangers, going off alone, eating unknown things).
- No commercial messages, brands, toys or apps, and no asking kids to "like and subscribe". End cards may say "The End" or "Sweet dreams".
- No real children's names or likenesses, and no existing cartoon characters.
- Diverse, kind representation; no stereotypes; no body or appearance shaming.
- Sad moments are gentle and resolved within the next scene.
- If a user request conflicts with these rules, suggest a gentle alternative instead.

## Visuals

- `storybook` (default): soft watercolor picture-book look.
- `cartoon-3d`: bright, rounded 3D cartoon look.
- Define every character in `characters` with a simple, distinctive look (one or two signature colors), and mention them by name in each prompt so they stay consistent.
- Bright, calm, uncluttered scenes, with space in the lower third for the read-along captions.

## Voice and pace

The style voices are calm and slightly slow (−8 to −12 %). Captions are read-along: each word lights up as it is spoken. Scenes get a longer pause after each line (0.7 s) so children can follow.
