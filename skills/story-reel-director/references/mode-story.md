# Mode: Story Narrator

Stories about anything: history, science, biographies, motivation, mysteries, true events, parables, "what if" scenarios, explainers told as a story.

## Story arcs (pick one and name it in the proposal)

1. **Hook → Context → Rising tension → Turn → Payoff** (default; biographies, history, events)
2. **Myth → Reality → Why it matters** (myth-busting, science)
3. **Problem → Struggle → Discovery → Lesson** (motivational, parables)
4. **Mystery → Clues → Reveal** (mysteries, "nobody knows why…")
5. **Countdown / list** ("5 inventions that…"): each item is one scene with a `fact` card

For long-form (3 min+), repeat the tension → turn beat every 60–90 seconds and add a mini-hook at each chapter start ("But that was only the beginning.").

## Staging (acted engine)

- Tell it through two or three characters with clear wants; let them speak instead of narrating everything.
- History and biography: a `child` or `adult` puppet stands in for the person in a simple costume (hat, glasses, scarf, colours). Abstract ideas (money, time, fear) can be floating characters.
- Use `time` changes and weather to show time passing; `night` + `push-in` for the low point, `day` + `pull-out` for the resolution.
- For realistic, cinematic or documentary looks, use the illustrated engine instead.

## Cards

- `title` on the hook scene.
- `fact` for one surprising number or claim the viewer should remember (at most one per 30 seconds).
- `end` on the last scene: a one-line takeaway plus a subtitle like "Follow for more stories".
- Optional `on_screen_text` (no card) for a punchy 2–4 word headline on a dramatic beat.

## Styles

- `cinematic`: default; history, drama, true stories.
- `sketch`: explainers, philosophy, business lessons.
- `anime`: adventure, fantasy, emotional and youth-oriented stories.

## Accuracy

Everything stated as fact must be true. For history and science, prefer well-documented events and round numbers. Mark legends as legends ("Legend says…"). When the user's source contains a doubtful claim, flag it in the proposal instead of narrating it.
